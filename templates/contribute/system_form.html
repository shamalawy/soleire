Where to find these numbers

Array size (kWp) — on your installation certificate, or panel wattage × number of panels ÷ 1000. Twelve 400 W panels is 4.8 kWp.

Inverter size (kW) — the model number usually contains it (a "SolarEdge SE5000" is 5 kW). Not the same as the array size.

Orientation — the direction the panel faces, not the direction of your front door. Split arrays have their own options.
