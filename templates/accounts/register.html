I already have a handle

Why you do not pick your own name

Given a username box, most people type something they already use — their own name, a work handle, the front half of their email. One field like that would tie a household to its electricity profile and undo the point of the whole dataset. So the site issues the handle instead, and never asks you for anything else.

What we store

  • Your handle, which means nothing on its own.
  • Your password, hashed with Argon2id — never the password itself.
  • The readings you file, published only as county aggregates.

No email, no name, no address, no IP logging tied to your account.
